About

I'm a Master's in Professional Accounting student at McCombs School of Business with a 3.81 GPA, graduating in May 2027. This past summer I interned at Apple on the Sales Finance team, building Tableau dashboards and automating quarter-end reporting for executives. Before college, I founded John's Painting Services, growing it to over 250 clients and a $10K+ peak month — and got featured on Fox-26 News for it. I've lived across 3 continents and 8 cities, including Stavanger, Norway; Lagos, Nigeria; and New York City — experiences that shaped how I think, connect, and move through the world. I care about finance, entrepreneurship, community service, and finding the best soccer field wherever I land.
